# Krabi coast: modern / 1967 comparison

Interactive transparency comparison, whole-coast and parcel-detail views, and three downloadable images.

- Historical imagery: USGS CORONA, 2 April 1967.
- Modern imagery: Esri mosaic; capture dates unverified. Credits: Esri, Vantor, Earthstar Geographics and GIS User Community.
- Blue: deed 47125, retaining the existing approximate outline.
- Red: 40802, 45591, 45592, 45594, 47124, 47128, 57183 and 75203.
- All outlines are saved approximate DOL LandsMaps screen traces, not surveyed boundary coordinates. September 2026 neighbour traces; November 2026 selected outline. No fresh title lookup is claimed.
- Historical alignment is approximate. Small offsets, gaps and overlaps are not proof of landscape change or legal adjacency.
- The 54240 / 50861 working sketch is not included as a DOL boundary.

Source parcel geometry and source periods are preserved in parcel-outlines.geojson. DOL reference: https://landsmaps.dol.go.th/

The page is static and has no sign-in requirement. No ownership, sale availability, title validity or building permission is certified by this map.
